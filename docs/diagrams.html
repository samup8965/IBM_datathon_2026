Solution flow

What happens every hour, from raw data to a risk map

flowchart TB
  START(["Every hour on LinuxONE"]) --> D["Download latest internet connectivity (IODA)
+ weather (Open-Meteo)"]
  D --> F["Build features per state and hour
recent outages · connectivity dips · heat · time · region"]
  F --> Q{"Already below 90%
of 2-week best?"}
  Q -- Yes --> NOW["Status: outage in progress"]
  Q -- No --> FC["Forecaster predicts chance of an outage
in the next 24 h · runs on Telum II (NNPA)"]
  FC --> LVL{"Risk level"}
  LVL --> LO["Low"]
  LVL --> MO["Moderate"]
  LVL --> SE["Severe"]
  LVL --> CR["Critical
outage follows 76% of the time"]
  NOW --> WHY["Explain why
e.g. recent cuts + connectivity dip"]
  LO --> WHY
  MO --> WHY
  SE --> WHY
  CR --> WHY
  WHY --> HH["Rank households to check first
risk × vulnerability"]
  HH --> KEEP["Keep risk, level and reasons in memory"]
  KEEP --> API["API serves the website and app instantly"]
  API --> APP["Website and app: map of Venezuela, risk per state,
reasons and what to do (household or medical)"]
  APP --> END(["Families prepare before the power goes out"])
